Short description

Thrown sixty-six million years into the past with nothing but a strange brass gauntlet, an explorer has to survive every era of history to find the way home. Chronoshift is a third-person action-survival game in Unreal Engine 5, from the age of the dinosaurs to the far future.

Long description

Chronoshift begins in a quarry, where a man finds a brass gauntlet buried in the rock, with a stone at its heart that still glows. When the stone answers a call for help from the far end of time, he falls sixty-six million years into the past.

Each of the game's thirteen eras is its own world, with its own creatures, weapons and dangers. Herds graze and drink, raptor packs hunt as a team, and wolves circle their prey. Players fight with spears, thrown stones and the atlatl, hide in the undergrowth, pick up power-ups that change how the hero fights, and find amber to take the form of a dinosaur. Every era hides three key pieces and a guardian, and beating them wakes the stone ring to the next age.

Chronoshift is made in the open by ITYELABS, a small independent studio, with a new devlog every week. Some concept art and 3D starting models are made with AI tools; all gameplay images come from the current build.
