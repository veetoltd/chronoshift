Era 2 · in development New

The first jump through time

Walk into the portal at the end of the Dinosaur Era and a cinematic carries you through the time tunnel into the Ice Age, with a new fur outfit for the hero. The frozen world now stretches 1.1 km: spruce taiga, a frozen lake, a mammoth steppe, rock ridges, hot springs and a glacier. There is no path. You find your way by landmarks, and the cold is an enemy of its own.

Every Ice Age area starts as concept art and a hand-drawn map, and the level is built to match them, side by side. These are the taiga trail paintings the forest was built from, clear and in a blizzard, and the map of the Ice Age as it stands today.

Dev log

How Chronoshift is coming together

Taken from the project's build history, week by week since the first commit.

8 to 9 Nov

The Ice Age grows: warmth, thin ice and the falcon

  • The Ice Age map grows to 1.1 km by 250 m: a frozen lake, a mammoth steppe, rock ridges and passes, hot springs, a glacier and snowy mountains on the skyline
  • Survival in the cold: a warmth meter that drains faster in a blizzard, refilled at campfires and hot pools, with frostbite damage when it runs out
  • Ice physics on the frozen lake: slide and skid on the ice, and thin patches crack and give way if you stand still or land hard
  • The falcon's eyrie: reach the nest and you can turn into a peregrine falcon, with flapping, gliding, banking turns and a tucked high-speed dive
  • Clearer air: lighter everyday snow and fog, so you can see across the lake to the mountains, with the real whiteouts saved for blizzards
  • The first pieces of the boss arena: a ring of braziers around the rim that you light with your torch
  • A full settings menu (DLSS, frame generation, Reflex, headphone audio and volumes), an in-game How to Play guide, and a new Windows installer for testers
7 Nov

A blizzard, real physics and a title screen

  • The taiga made photoreal: frosted spruces, a trampled snow trail with rocks and deadwood, softer winter light
  • The first snowstorm: driving snow, gusting wind and howling audio that slows the hero down
  • Creatures react to hits, wolf tails swing as they run, and the hero braces when armour stops a bite
  • Footprints and paw tracks press into the snow and slowly fill back in
  • Deadwood logs splinter when you swing at them, and young spruces bend as you push through
  • A title screen with the new logo art and our song, Cross the Ages
6 Nov

Ice Age wolves and a new trailer

  • The first Ice Age enemy: a dire wolf pack, with 15 animations from a stalking crouch to a galloping lunge
  • 28 new wolf sounds: lone howls, a distant pack chorus, snarls, bites and paws in the snow
  • A new cinematic trailer, from the hero's lab to the age of dinosaurs and the eras beyond
4 – 5 Nov

Into the Ice Age

  • The portal works: walk in, ride the time tunnel, arrive in the Ice Age
  • The hero's outfit changes with every era, starting with hides and furs
  • A snowy taiga with wind-swayed spruces, falling powder, boot prints, kicked-up snow and footstep crunch
  • The volcano cave rebuilt, with a wall-crawling giant scorpion and three gas vents: scalding jets, sulphur clouds and choking fog
  • Three new Temporal Log entries
2 – 3 Nov

Matching the concept art

  • The jungle edge rebuilt to the concept painting: skyline volcano, smoke plume, god rays and valley mist
  • The volcano canyon rebuilt with a live eruption system and a lava bridge
  • The original dinosaur skins brought back, plus creature and prop fixes
27 – 29 Oct

New dangers and a second boss

  • A jungle gorge, and four new jungle hazards: the Living Log, Tiger-Jaws, steam vents and rubble slides
  • The Constrictor Vine, a braided liana that snares the hero, and Arthropleura, a giant millipede ambusher
  • A rebuilt volcanic end level: lava bridge, slab arena and an eruption that builds through the fight
  • A Pachycephalosaurus end boss, with a tyrannosaur prowling the ridge above
24 – 26 Oct

Smarter, louder dinosaurs

  • Raptor pack hunting, and 236 new enemy animations
  • Full animation sets for the Triceratops, the Parasaurolophus and the flying reptiles
  • A unique ElevenLabs sound set for all 15 dinosaur species
  • Dinosaurs walk to water to drink, and plants bend as they pass
  • A reworked meadow, a cliff-and-river valley, a coastal ridge, and a stone-framed HUD
19 – 23 Oct

Powers, morphs and the first trailers

  • New looks for Fury, Adrenaline, Armour, Shadow and Regeneration, and glowing pickups
  • Swamp dangers: sinking mud, tar, geysers and a rockfall trap
  • Dinosaurs got more attack moves; the bola got hold-to-aim
  • Melee now turns the hero to the nearest enemy, and the target lights up
  • Two trailers rendered in-engine, plus the cover art
12 – 16 Oct

Rex Prime, rebuilt

  • A new tyrannosaur with seven animations: idle, walk, run, roar, bite, hit and death
  • Twelve new sounds, a heavy walk cycle and a head that tracks the player
  • Volcano eruption, a dusk sky and an overhauled boss arena
  • A settings menu with resolution and quality options
4 – 9 Oct

A living world

  • Ferns, reeds and cycads move in the wind
  • The hero wades through the swamp instead of walking on water
  • Three key pieces are needed to wake the portal
  • Environment meshes optimised from 168 MB to 14 MB
29 Sep – 2 Oct

Flyers and the Temporal Log

  • New species: Psittacosaurus and Archaeoceratops
  • Flying reptiles that bank, pitch, land and feed
  • The Temporal Log: a bestiary that fills in as you discover each creature
  • Richer colour across the level, and the first sound pass
22 – 27 Sep

Dangers and the boss

  • Lava rivers, steam vents, burning damage and thorn thickets
  • Hit animations for every dinosaur, six new jungle species and a Spinosaurus
  • Rex Prime gets his arena; campfires and torches light the way
18 – 21 Sep

Building the Dinosaur Era

  • A sculpted landscape with seven zones: jungle trail, meadow, swamp, canyon, ash fields, lava plains and the arena
  • Animated dinosaurs, and a brontosaurus herd in the meadow
  • The Era 1 hero replaces the stand-in mannequin
  • Spear and club in hand, plus crouch-stealth that shrinks detection to 45%
16 Sep

Day one

  • First commit of the Era 1 project
