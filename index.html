Why Chronoshift

One explorer. Every age of the Earth.

Survival games usually give you one world. Chronoshift gives you all of history, one era at a time, and every jump changes what you fight, how you hide and what you can become.

Thirteen eras

Thirteen worlds in one game

Not one setting but the whole of history: dinosaur jungles, the frozen Ice Age, the Bronze Age, Ancient Egypt and on to the far future. Every era is its own world, with its own creatures, weapons and ways to die.

Morph

Become the beast

Find amber and take the form of the creatures you are running from. Hunt as a Raptor, charge as a Triceratops, glide over the valley as a Pteranodon.

Living world

A world that does not wait for you

Herds graze and drink at the river, raptor packs hunt as a team and scatter when they lose their nerve, wolves circle their prey. You are part of the food chain, not the centre of it.

Guardians

Every era ends with a giant

Each era is guarded by something the timeline has twisted: Rex Prime and the Pachycephalosaurus in the volcanic arena, then the woolly rhino, the steppe mammoth, the Elder Megatherium and the giant bird Aiolornis in the Ice Age.

The game

Survive every era of history

Chronoshift is a third-person action-survival game. You play an explorer flung back through time by a strange brass gauntlet, and the only way home is forward: through thirteen eras, from the age of the dinosaurs to the far future.

Explore

Living worlds

Each era is its own world with its own creatures and dangers. Herds drink at the river, raptor packs hunt together, the jungle hides vine snares and tar pits, and the Ice Age brings blizzards, thin lake ice and the cold itself.

Survive

Fight, hide or run

Spears, thrown stones and the atlatl. Crouch in the undergrowth to slip past predators, pick up power-ups that change how you fight, and find amber to take the form of a dinosaur.

Escape

Three keys and a guardian

Every era hides three key pieces and the guardians at its heart, from Rex Prime in the volcanic arena to the giants of the Ice Age. Beat them and the stone ring wakes, sending you on to the next age.

The story New

The Gauntlet

How an ordinary man ended up sixty-six million years from home. The animated version, Chronoshift: An Adventure in Time, is on the videos page.

Chapter 1 · The find

It began in a quarry.

Buried in the rock, older than the rock itself, was a brass gauntlet. Set in its heart was a stone that still glowed. He took it home: brass and gears, made by no hand he knew, and inside the stone, something pulsed.

Chapter 2 · The call

It was a signal.

A call for help, repeating, from the far end of time. Then one night, the stone answered.

Chapter 3 · The fall

The gauntlet was a key.

Somewhere in history, a door had opened. He came out sixty-six million years in the past, and the gauntlet dressed him for the world he had fallen into.

Chapter 4 · The rings

A chain of doors through time.

The stone rings were built long ago, by a people no one remembers. Each era holds the power the gauntlet needs, and amber keeps the memory of the beasts.

Chapter 5 · The guardians

Three key pieces in every era.

Every source of power is guarded by something the timeline has twisted. In every era, three key pieces lie hidden. Find them all, and the next ring wakes.

Chapter 6 · The road

Every ring takes him further from home.

From the age of the dinosaurs, through the Ice Age, to the very end of time, he has to keep moving. Who sent the call? And will he ever find his way back?

How we make it

Built in the open, week by week

Chronoshift is made by ITYELABS, a small independent studio (Veeto Ltd), with the Console and PC Gaming channel following every step. Here is what we are doing and how.

Unreal Engine 5

The game runs in Unreal Engine 5, with photoreal lighting, scanned rock and plant assets, physics for ice, water and foliage, and creature AI that herds, hunts, drinks and flees.

Every creature brought to life

Twenty-one species so far. Each one is modelled, rigged and given its own animations, sounds and behaviour, then checked in the game against its concept art.

One era at a time

The Dinosaur Era is playable from the jungle to the volcanic arena and the portal. The Ice Age is being built now, and the eras after it follow in order.

A devlog every week

Every week of work goes into the devlog as text and screenshots, and onto YouTube as an episode, so you can watch the game grow from an empty project.

We use AI tools and say so: some 3D models start as AI generations before rigging and animation, concept and story art is AI-assisted, our music is made with Suno, and creature sounds and narration use ElevenLabs. Gameplay images and in-engine trailers come from the current build.

Power-ups

The past has its gifts

Glowing pickups hidden through the level. Each one changes how the hero looks as well as how he fights, so you can read a buff at a glance.

Power-up art: concept illustrations made with AI image tools. In the game, each power changes the hero's look in the same way.

Fury

Blood on fire

A molten glow over body and weapon, and every blow lands harder.

Adrenaline

Faster than lightning

Electric skin, sparks at every footfall and a burst of speed.

Armour

Living bronze

Turns the hero to a bronze statue that shrugs off bites and claws.

Shadow

Hide from every eye

A glassy shimmer the predators look straight through.

Regeneration

Heal what should kill

A green glow that restores health over time, even mid-fight.

Morph

Find the amber. Become the beast.

Amber pickups let the hero take the form of a dinosaur, each with its own moves.

02Ice AgeIn development View progress

Walk into the portal at the end of the Dinosaur Era and a cinematic carries you through the time tunnel into the Ice Age, with a new fur outfit for the hero. The frozen world stretches 1.1 km: spruce taiga, a frozen lake, a mammoth steppe, rock ridges, hot springs and a glacier. There is no path. You find your way by landmarks, and the cold is an enemy of its own.

Built

  • The 1.1 km frozen landscape, lake, ridges, glacier and mountain skyline
  • Photoreal taiga, snowstorms, wind and snow that takes your footprints
  • The dire wolf pack
  • Warmth and campfires, ice physics and thin ice
  • The falcon eyrie, and flying as a peregrine falcon
  • The giant ground sloth and the short-faced bear, with fur that sways in the wind
  • The atlatl spear-thrower, and a map twice as wide with a steppe bay and mountain backdrop
  • Four giant bosses: a woolly rhino, a steppe mammoth bull, the Elder Megatherium and Aiolornis, the largest flying bird ever known
  • A wolf pack that hunts as a team, and a spear you can aim and brace against a charge
  • Lake ice that shatters under you, and climbing back out of the freezing water

15 to 17 Nov

Four Ice Age giants

  • Four new bosses, each with its own animations, sounds and fighting style: a woolly rhino that charges on sight, a steppe mammoth bull, the Elder Megatherium (the oldest and largest ground sloth) and Aiolornis, the largest flying bird ever known, attacking from the sky

  • Fall through the lake ice into the freezing water below, and haul yourself back out onto the ice

Ice that breaks, and a new fur outfit

  • Lake ice that cracks and breaks into floating slabs, with slush and mist on the open water
  • A new Ice Age outfit for the hero, with a fur mantle and coat that sway as he moves
  • Spear aiming: an aim arc, a crosshair and a lock-on marker, and you can brace the spear against a charging animal
10 to 11 Nov

Ice Age giants: the ground sloth and the short-faced bear

  • The giant ground sloth (Megatherium), built from our concept art: it grazes calmly, rears up to warn you off, charges if you push it, and sleeps
  • The short-faced bear (Arctodus) rebuilt from its concept art, with snow-crusted fur, a towering size next to the hero, and a carcass it guards and feeds on
  • Fur that moves: both coats ripple in the wind, harder in a blizzard, and their hanging belly and arm fur swings as they walk
  • The atlatl, an Ice Age spear-thrower for the hero
  • The map is now twice as wide, with a new steppe bay and a mountain backdrop
  • Fixes: thin white lines on the hero's furs, and an unnatural snow wall beside the trail that is now a natural slope
8 to 9 Nov

The Ice Age grows: warmth, thin ice and the falcon

  • The Ice Age map grows to 1.1 km by 250 m: a frozen lake, a mammoth steppe, rock ridges and passes, hot springs, a glacier and snowy mountains on the skyline
  • Survival in the cold: a warmth meter that drains faster in a blizzard, refilled at campfires and hot pools, with frostbite damage when it runs out
  • Ice physics on the frozen lake: slide and skid on the ice, and thin patches crack and give way if you stand still or land hard
  • The falcon's eyrie: reach the nest and you can turn into a peregrine falcon, with flapping, gliding, banking turns and a tucked high-speed dive
  • Clearer air: lighter everyday snow and fog, so you can see across the lake to the mountains, with the real whiteouts saved for blizzards
  • The first pieces of the boss arena: a ring of braziers around the rim that you light with your torch
  • A full settings menu (DLSS, frame generation, Reflex, headphone audio and volumes), an in-game How to Play guide, and a new Windows installer for testers
7 Nov

A blizzard, real physics and a title screen

  • The taiga made photoreal: frosted spruces, a trampled snow trail with rocks and deadwood, softer winter light
  • The first snowstorm: driving snow, gusting wind and howling audio that slows the hero down
  • Creatures react to hits, wolf tails swing as they run, and the hero braces when armour stops a bite
  • Footprints and paw tracks press into the snow and slowly fill back in
  • Deadwood logs splinter when you swing at them, and young spruces bend as you push through
  • A title screen with the new logo art and our song, Cross the Ages
6 Nov

Ice Age wolves and a new trailer

  • The first Ice Age enemy: a dire wolf pack, with 15 animations from a stalking crouch to a galloping lunge
  • 28 new wolf sounds: lone howls, a distant pack chorus, snarls, bites and paws in the snow
  • A new cinematic trailer, from the hero's lab to the age of dinosaurs and the eras beyond

01Prehistoric · the Dinosaur EraPlayable · polishing View progress

The Dinosaur Era is playable end to end. Start on a jungle trail and work through the meadow, the swamp, the canyon and the lava plains to the volcano, two bosses and the portal out of time.

Built

  • Seven zones: jungle trail, meadow, swamp, canyon, ash fields, lava plains and the volcano
  • 15 dinosaur species, each with its own animations and sound set
  • Raptor packs that hunt together, flyers that bank, land and feed
  • A living ecosystem: dinosaurs get hungry, thirsty and tired, predators hunt, and herds graze and drink
  • A wilder jungle, with plants that sway in the wind and part as you push through, and footsteps that change with the ground
  • A 36-metre waterfall in the jungle gorge, with a plunge pool and rapids down to the river
  • Two bosses: Rex Prime and a Pachycephalosaurus, in a volcanic arena that erupts as you fight
  • Hazards: lava, tar, sinking mud, geysers, rockfalls, snaring vines and ambush predators
  • Five powers, three animal morphs and the Temporal Log bestiary
  • The volcano cave, and the portal to the Ice Age

Now

  • Polishing: performance, balance and the last visual fixes
  • Finishing the waterfall: churning white water and rising mist where it lands, and wet rock
